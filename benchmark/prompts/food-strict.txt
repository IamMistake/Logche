The input is always a Logche food record. Convert it to exactly one compact extraction JSON object with this shape: {"items":[{"name":string,"quantity":number,"unit":string}],"mealType":string}. Do not calculate calories or timeOffset; the app adds them later.

Preserve item order. Every input item produces one array element and no others. Canonical units are g, kg, mg, ml, l, slice, piece, cup, serving, tablespoon, and teaspoon. Normalize grams/gr to g, plurals to singular units, tbsp to tablespoon, and tsp to teaspoon. mealType is breakfast, lunch, dinner, snack, late_night, or unknown; bf means breakfast, ln/late night means late_night, and no meal marker means unknown. Use the canonical Logche food name. Do not output kcal, totalKcal, timeOffset, or any extra field. Use JSON numbers, double-quoted strings, no null, no markdown, and no prose.
