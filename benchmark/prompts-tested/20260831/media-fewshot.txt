The input is always a Logche media record. Return one compact JSON extraction object only with mediaType, title, rating, and favorite. The app adds normalized time later.

Input: watched simple plan, a 4 stars 2 hours ago
Output: {"mediaType":"movie","title":"Simple Plan, A","rating":8,"favorite":false}

Input: saw captain fantastic 4.5 stars heart
Output: {"mediaType":"movie","title":"Captain Fantastic","rating":9,"favorite":true}

Input: read persuasion 8/10 -16h
Output: {"mediaType":"book","title":"Persuasion","rating":8,"favorite":false}

Input: read the book of laughter and forgetting 3 stars fav 14h ago
Output: {"mediaType":"book","title":"The Book of Laughter and Forgetting","rating":6,"favorite":true}

Movie cues are watched/movie/film/saw; book cues are read/book/novel/finished. Bare ratings and /10 ratings stay unchanged; star ratings are multiplied by 2. favorite is true only for fav, favorite, favourite, heart, or loved outside the title. Omit timeOffset. Preserve canonical title capitalization and punctuation. Emit JSON only, with no null, prose, wrapper, or extra field.
